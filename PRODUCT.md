# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Admins create employee accounts and assign tasks. Employees sign in to review and manage their assigned tasks.

## Product Purpose

The app provides a place for admins to manage employee accounts and tasks, and for employees to track the status of their assigned work.

## Operating Context

Admins create tasks for employees. Employees can accept, complete, or mark assigned tasks as failed. The app currently stores employee, task, and session data in the browser's local storage.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Admin and employee sign-in.
- Admins can add employees and create tasks assigned to employees.
- Employees can accept, complete, or fail tasks.
- Task totals reflect new, active, completed, and failed tasks.
- The redesign must preserve existing features and workflows; it must not add or remove functionality.

## Brand Commitments

- The user requests a classic, professional UI.
- Do not use gradients, blue, or purple.

## Product Principles

- Preserve both admin and employee workflows.
- Keep task status and account information easy to understand.
- Preserve locally stored data and existing user-facing behavior.
